🎭 Inside the Studio

About Jumbiko Studio

Jumbiko Studio (@jumbikostudio) crafts original Hindi animated stories, gentle bedtime fables, and expressive cartoon comedy designed for children and multi-generational families in India and across the globe.

Every episode blends warm Indian storytelling values—kindness, honesty, curiosity, and family togetherness—with approachable bilingual titles and captions so kids everywhere can enjoy and learn together.
